# Ballot Box Rush

A one-life platform game. Guide Boxy the ballot box as far as you can along the campaign trail: up, down, left and right.

## Play
Open `index.html` in a browser. Everything is in that one file.

## Controls
- **Phone:** ◀ ▶ to move, tap the screen or JUMP to jump, tap the screen to shoot in boss fights, RALLY/VETO when the meter is full (100 ballots).
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD, Space to jump, F to fire, R for rally/veto, P or Esc to pause.

## Features
- Zones in every direction, with ladders, ropes, bouncy platforms, crumbling and moving stages
- Enemies: lobbyists, flying lobbyists and hecklers that throw NO-vote letters
- Portals to Mega Lobbyist boss fights with a rage phase, 3 hearts for Boxy, and arena power-ups that give the sidekick special attacks
- Power-ups, rally meter, one revive per boss win
- A tiny sidekick (blond quiff, blue suit) who walks and jumps along behind Boxy
- Shop with Boxy skins (trail effects) and 8 sidekicks: Mr. Quiff, The Mayor, The Minister, The Activist, The Speaker, Campaign Boss, Robo Candidate, Floating Voter
- Skin shop with trail effects, daily challenges, best scores, sound and music

Progress (wallet, skins, sidekicks, records) is saved in your browser. Use **SAVE CODE** in the menu to copy it to another phone or browser.
